Frequently asked questions about Gạo Beauty Tan Phu

Where is Gạo Beauty located in Tan Phu District?+

The salon is at 012D, Nhieu Loc Apartment, Phu Tho Hoa Ward, Ho Chi Minh City (formerly Tan Quy Ward, Tan Phu District) — right opposite Tan Huong Primary School. Very close to Tan Quy, Hoa Thanh and Son Ky.

How much do eyelash extensions cost in Tan Phu?+

Eyelash extensions at Gạo Beauty range from 200,000₫ to 380,000₫ depending on style and thickness (Classic, Volume, Mega Volume). Free consultation before the service.

What nail services does Gạo Beauty offer?+

Regular polish 30,000₫, gel polish 80,000₫, dipping powder 90,000₫, cat-eye/Flash polish 100,000₫–130,000₫, nail extensions 80,000₫–110,000₫, nail art and rhinestones from 10,000₫/nail. Cuticle trimming and heel scrubbing also available.

Can the Tan Phu nail salon do custom nail-art designs?+

Yes. Gạo Beauty can recreate a design you bring in or suggest one suited to your hand shape, skin tone and occasion: hand-painted art, ombre, rhinestones, mirror finish, 3D flowers. Designs are refreshed seasonally, suitable for both everyday wear and special events.

How long does gel polish last in Tan Phu, and does it damage natural nails?+

Gel polish at Gạo Beauty lasts about 2–3 weeks depending on your activities. Technicians gently prep the nail surface and remove gel through proper soaking, minimizing damage to natural nails. We recommend a short gel-free break between applications to let nails recover.

What does a relaxing hair wash at the salon in Tan Phu include?+
