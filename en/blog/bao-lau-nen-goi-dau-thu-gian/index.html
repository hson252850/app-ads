How often should you get a relaxing hair wash at a salon?

A relaxing hair wash at a salon differs from washing at home in that it adds scalp exfoliation, a nourishing hair mask, and deep head–shoulder–neck massage. Since it takes more time and costs more than a regular wash, many clients wonder how often is actually worth it.

1. Oily, easily flat scalp: once a week

An oily scalp gets greasy fast and easily traps dust and buildup, clogging the scalp. A relaxing wash combined with exfoliation about once a week helps deep-clean, reducing scalp breakouts and hair loss caused by clogged follicles.

2. Dry or normal scalp: every 2 weeks

For a scalp that isn't particularly oily, a relaxing wash every 2 weeks is reasonable — enough for a deep clean and regular relaxing massage without stripping the scalp's natural protective oils.

3. After perming, coloring or straightening

Recently chemically-treated hair is usually weaker and the scalp more sensitive. Wait about 3-5 days after a chemical treatment before getting a relaxing wash, and choose a gentle herbal wash rather than strong exfoliation during this period.

4. When you're often stressed or not sleeping well

Head–shoulder–neck massage relaxes the nervous system and can support better sleep. If work stress is ongoing, you can increase frequency to once a week even if your scalp isn't oily, mainly for mental relaxation rather than deep cleaning.

5. Signs your scalp is "asking for help" right now

  • Persistent itching or unusually heavy dandruff.
  • Noticeably more hair falling out when brushing or washing.
  • A frequent feeling of heaviness in the head or tension in the shoulders and neck.
